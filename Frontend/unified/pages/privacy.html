Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 30, 2026

Overview

Verdanist Conservatory ("Verdanist," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our smart greenhouse management platform, website, and related services.

By using Verdanist services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. We encourage you to read this document carefully and contact us if you have any questions.

Information We Collect

person Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you create an account:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials (encrypted passwords)
  • Profile information (farm name, location)
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment providers)

sensors Agricultural Data

Our platform collects data from your greenhouse systems:

  • Sensor readings (temperature, humidity, soil moisture, light levels)
  • Irrigation system logs and schedules
  • Zone configurations and thresholds
  • Crop information and growth data
  • System performance metrics

devices Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and location data
  • Usage patterns and preferences
  • Log data and error reports

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

settings

Service Provision

To operate and maintain your greenhouse management system, process automation commands, and deliver real-time monitoring capabilities.

analytics

Analytics

To analyze usage patterns, improve our services, develop new features, and provide insights about your agricultural operations.

notifications

Communication

To send important alerts about your greenhouse, system notifications, and respond to your inquiries and support requests.

security

Security

To detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other security incidents, and to ensure compliance with legal obligations.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • check_circle
    Service Providers: We share data with trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating our platform (hosting, payment processing, analytics).
  • check_circle
    Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • check_circle
    Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred as part of the business transaction.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Specific retention periods vary by data type:

  • Account Information: Retained while account is active; deleted 30 days after account closure
  • Sensor Data: Retained for 24 months (premium plans) or 12 months (basic plans)
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years per legal requirements
  • Log Files: Retained for 90 days
